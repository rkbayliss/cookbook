## Preparation
1. Mandolin a bunch of carrots.
2. Heat 1.5 tablespoons of olive oil in a pan over medium low heat
3. Pour in the carrots. Your goal is to sort of softly caramelize/braise them in the mustard and honey. The carrots will release their liquids creating a nice sauce
