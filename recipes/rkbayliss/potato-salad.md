## Katherine
I never liked potato salad growing up, but at Rod's fraternity ND, the cook Dona Vicki (DV) made the best potato salad I've ever had. Since then, I'd occasionally make potato salad, but once my mom and Josie got a taste of it, it became one of my most requested recipes. 

## Ingredients & Equipment
- Big Pot with Lid
- Big Bowl
- Golden potatoes
- VLassic Sweet Relish
- Mayonaise
- 1/4 white onion or shallot
- 1 egg (medium hard boiled)

## Preparation
- Fill the large pot with water leaving enough room for the potatoes to be added. You want enough water so the potatoes end up with about 1" of water above them. Salt the water generously. The salt in the water is proportional to the amount of water in the pot, not the number of potatoes. You want a salty solution so the potatoes are salted from within as they cook.
- Peel and cut potatoes into 3/4" chunks. Precision isn't important here, but cutting into small pieces reduces cooking time and consistent size ensures consistent cooking.
