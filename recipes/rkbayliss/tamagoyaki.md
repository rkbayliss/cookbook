## From Katherine
I make this for Rod somewhat often. It's so easy to whip up and always hits. 

## Ingredients
- Eggs
- 1/4 white onion
- chicken thighs (ideally boneless & skinless)
- sake
- soy sauce
- broth (chicken, dashi, boullion are all acceptable here)
- white sugar
- cooked jasmine rice (for serving)

## Equipment
- Shallow pan w/ lid
- Scissors

## Preparation
1. Thinly slice the onion from
2. Place the white onion in a sauce pan over low/med heat
3. Add sake, soy sauce, and broth. Simmer broth.
4. Use scissors to cut small chunks of chicken into the broth.
5. Whisk eggs and, once chicken is mostly cooked, pour eggs over top
6. Place lid on the pan and let simmer until eggs are just set
7. Serve in a bowl served over jasmine rice.
