## From Katherine
Jeremy made this for Cheyenne and Luis' wedding pot luck. As the legend goes, he blacked out the night before and didn't remember putting it in the oven, but black-out Jeremy had done sober Jeremy a solid. It was delicious. The following week, I made it for Ray for his birthday. It's pretty easy to make and incredibly aromatic. Your home will smell insane. I'd like to try and modify it to work in a slow cooker. 

## Ingredients

- 10 lbs pork shoulder blade roast ("Boston butt")
- Kosher salt, for salting the meat

**For the marinade**

- 1/3 cup packed brown sugar
- 1 tbsp + 1 tsp mild chili powder
- 1 tbsp + 1 tsp paprika
- 2 3/4 tsp garlic powder
- 2 3/4 tsp kosher salt
- 1 1/3 tsp ground black pepper
- 1 1/3 tsp cayenne (or a bit extra)

**To finish**

- 5 yellow onions
- BBQ sauce

## Preparation

1. **Wash and dry:** wash the meat, then dry it well.
2. **Salt:** salt the meat and let it sit for 1 hour.
3. **Marinate:** mix the brown sugar, chili powder, paprika, garlic powder, salt, black pepper, and cayenne, and coat the meat all over. Put it in the Dutch oven and refrigerate overnight.
4. **Onions:** cut the onions in half and line the bottom of the Dutch oven with them, cut side down. Put the meat on top, fat side up, so the fat renders down through the meat.
5. **Cook:** put it in the oven at 300°F and set an alarm for 6 hours later.
6. **Reduce:** take the meat out of the Dutch oven and reduce the sauce.
7. **Pull:** the meat should fall right apart. Pull it, then mix in some BBQ sauce and the very soft, juicy onions.

## Notes

- **From Jeremy:** "Yellow onions go underneath cut side down so it stacks on top, and fat side up so as it renders the fat slides through the meat."
- **November 2026 (Katherine's first try):** salted by feel before marinating and it came out a little too salty, so go lighter on the salt. Our yellow onions were enormous and five didn't fit; use as many halves as cover the bottom of the pot.
