Goes with the [[brined-sous-vide-turkey|Brined Sous Vide Turkey]]. No exact measurements here; this is the idea. The stock uses up the carcass, the neck, and the liquid from the sous vide bags, and the gravy is a bacon fat roux thinned with that turkey liquid.

## Ingredients

**Stock**

- Turkey carcass (left over after butchering)
- Turkey neck (removed when you prep the turkey)
- Water, enough to cover
- Leftover cooking liquid from the sous vide bags

**Gravy**

- Fat (bacon fat)
- Flour
- Cooking liquid from one of the sous vide bags

## Preparation

**Stock**

1. Put the turkey carcass and neck in a slow cooker, cover them with water, and cook on low.
2. When the sous vide turkey is done, pour the extra liquid from the bags into the slow cooker too.

**Gravy**

1. Make a roux: heat the bacon fat and whisk in the flour.
2. Deglaze with cooking liquid from one of the sous vide bags, stirring until smooth.

## Notes

- The stock isn't for the gravy; it's just to have. Freeze it and use it during the year.
- The basic formula for gravy is fat, flour, and cooking liquid. Any fat works; bacon fat is what was used here.
