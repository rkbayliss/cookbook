Thanksgiving 2026 turkey: brined with Kinder's Butcher's Brine Kit, then broken down and cooked sous vide, with the light and dark meat at different temperatures, and finished under the broiler.

## Ingredients

- 1 whole turkey, up to 20 lbs
- 1 Kinder's Butcher's Brine Kit (brine mix, Buttery Poultry Blend Seasoning, brining bag, twist tie)
- 8 cups water, for boiling
- 8 cups ice
- 8 cups cold water
- 5 sous vide bags

**Aromatics, for each breast and leg quarter bag**

- 1/4 Honeycrisp apple
- 1/4 onion
- 1/2 cinnamon stick
- Fresh sage
- Fresh rosemary
- Fresh thyme
- 1 clove fresh garlic, smashed

## Preparation

1. **Thaw:** thaw the turkey in the refrigerator, allowing 1 day for every 4 to 5 lbs. Once completely thawed, remove the neck and giblets and make sure the cavity is empty. Save the neck for stock.
2. **Make the brine:** in a large pot, bring 8 cups of water to a boil. Turn off the heat and stir in the brine mix until fully dissolved. Add 8 cups of ice and 8 cups of cold water, and stir until the ice dissolves.
3. **Brine:** line a large pot or container with the brining bag and pour in the brine. Put the turkey in the bag breast side down. Pull up the edges, twist closed while pushing out the excess air, and seal tightly with the twist tie. Refrigerate overnight for 12 hours (6 hours if the turkey is under 12 lbs).
4. **Dry:** remove the turkey from the bag and pat it dry. Throw away the brine and the bag.
5. **Butcher:** cut the turkey into 2 airline breasts (breast with the drumette still attached), 2 leg quarters (thigh and drumstick), and the rest of the wings. That makes 5 bags: one for each breast, one for each leg quarter, and one for the wings.
6. **Season:** rub each piece with the Buttery Poultry Blend Seasoning from the kit.
7. **Bag:** put each piece in its own sous vide bag, then add a quarter of a Honeycrisp apple, a quarter of an onion, half a cinnamon stick, fresh sage, fresh rosemary, fresh thyme, and a smashed clove of garlic. Put the aromatics on the flesh side of the turkey, not the skin side. The wings bag gets whatever aromatics are left over, usually the apple core and a little of everything.
8. **Sous vide:** cook the breasts and wings at 130°F and the leg quarters at 150°F, for 12 to 24 hours.
9. **Brown:** take the pieces out of the bags and put them skin side up on a baking sheet. Low broil to brown the skin. Watch closely, and rotate the pieces and the tray as needed so the browning is even. You may want to cut off the drumettes and drumsticks and brown them separately.
10. **Carve:** cut off the drumette and drumstick. Slice the breast against the grain. For the thigh, remove the bone, then slice against the grain.

## Notes

- For crispier skin, make sure the turkey is completely dry. The brine kit suggests air drying it uncovered in the refrigerator for up to 2 days.
- The brine kit box's own method roasts the whole turkey instead: brush with butter or oil, rub with the Buttery Poultry Blend Seasoning, and roast breast side up at 325°F (rack on the lowest shelf), about 1 hour per 4 lbs, until a thermometer reads at least 165°F. Rest 30 minutes before carving.
- Food safety: 130°F is the very bottom of the safe range for sous vide poultry. It's only safe because of the long cook time, and the water must stay at or above 130°F the whole time. Many sous vide cooks use about 135°F to 140°F for light meat for more margin.
- Save the carcass, the neck, and the liquid from the bags for the [[turkey-gravy-and-carcass-stock|Turkey Gravy and Carcass Stock]].
- Compare with [[sabrinas-thanksgiving-turkey|Sabrina's Thanksgiving Turkey]] from the 2012 Paseman cookbook, which uses similar aromatics.
