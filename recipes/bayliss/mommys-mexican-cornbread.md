## Ingredients

- 1 ½ Cup corn meal
- ⅓ cup Wesson Oil
- Dash garlic powder (big)
- 2 eggs
- ½ cup bell pepper
- 1 cup sour cream
- ½ cup onion
- 3-6 jalapeno peppers, chopped
- 1½ to 2 cup cheddar cheese, grated

## Preparation

1. Heat oven to 450 F
2. Mix all the ingredients and pout in a well greased baking pan
3. Sprinkle the cheese on top
4. Bake 20-30 minutes or until done
