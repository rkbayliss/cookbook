## From Rod and Katherine

## Ingredients

- Couple pounds of ham hocks
- Couple stalks of greens

## Preparation

1. Render the ham hock. Add ham hock (whole) to pot of water then boil then turn down heat to medium-high (ensure it continually boils). Render at medium-high for 1-2 hours. Make sure to keep water from drying out
2. Wash and prepare collards. Wash leaves as bought. Remove "spine" from leaves. Prepare to julienne leaves: Stack leaves on top of one another, roll into a roll, chop roll into lengths of ¾' to 1". Wash with soap and water (twice). Wash very well
3. Add washed greens to stock pot of water and ham hock. Continue boiling ham hock and greens. This step finishes when greens are tender. Time depends on green and amount but finishes when greens are tender. Could add a whole pepper during this stage.
4. Add sugar (tsp-Tbsp) to finished greens. Sugar is to cut bitterness not to make greens "sweet".  Can also add salt, chicken bouillon, or chicken base (choose 1).
