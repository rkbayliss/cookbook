## From Rod and Katherine

## Ingredients

- 2 cups sugar
- 1 cup wesson oil
- 2 eggs
- 2 bottles (1-ounce each) red food coloring
- 2 ½ cups sifted cake flour
- 2 tablespoons cocoa
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1 cup buttermilk
- 1 tablespoon white vinegar
- 1.5 tsp vanille

**Cream cheese frosting**

- 8-ounce package cream package cream cheese
- ½ cup margarine, softened
- 1 tsp vanilla
- 1-pound box confectioner's sugar (sift if lumpy)
- 1 cup chopped pecans

## Preparation

1. Preheat oven to 350 F.
2. Sift together flour, cocoa, salt and soda
3. Cream sugar and butter until fluffy
4. Add eggs, one at a time, beating 1 minute after each addition
5. Mix buttermilk with vinegar, adding to first mixture alternately with the dry ingredients
6. Beat on medium speed of mixer until well mixed
7. Add food coloring
8. Turn into 3 greased, waxpaper lined 9-inch cake pans
9. Bake for 35 to 40 minutes
10. Cool
11. Spread frosting between layers and over top and sides of cake

**Cream cheese frosting**

1. Blend all ingredients together well
2. Spread on cake
