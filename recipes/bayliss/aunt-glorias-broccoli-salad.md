## From Rod and Katherine

## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup sugar
- 1/2 cup halved cherry tomatoes
- 2 Tablespoons of white vinegar
- 1 cup Mayonnaise
- 1/2 cup Sun-Maid golden raisins
- 1/2 cup finely chopped red onions
- 6-8 slices cooked bacon
- 1 head broccoli or 1 bag of broccoli florets (wash and pat real dry).

## Preparation

1. Cook bacon
2. Chop red onions very fine (I use a chopper)and set aside
3. Mix Mayonnaise, sugar and white vinegar - well with a spoon.
4. Add onion and stir together
5. Add golden raisins and stir (raisins are optional. I like with and without)
6. Add broccoli and mix well with all the other ingredients.
7. Stir in bacon and mix well
8. Add some shredded cheeddar cheese and mix - if you like. (I did not when you all were here)
9. Add cherry tomatoes and mix together.
10. Chill/refrigerate for about 3 hours or longer for the taste to "set in". Sometimes I make and let sit overnight.
