Aunt B used to make these

## Ingredients

- Yams (real potatoes)
- Cinnamon
- Nutmeg
- Allspice
- Real Vanilla
- ½ Butter stick
- ½ Margarine Stick
- White Sugar

## Preparation

1. Prepare the yams. Cut the ends off and wash them really good. Put the candied yams in a boiler and boil for about 20-25 minutes (don't cook them all the way) (but they should be pokable)
2. Cool and peel the yams. Take them out of the water, let them cool off, then peel the yams. Slice the yams in ¼" slices
3. Preheat the oven to 350° F.
4. Prepare the casserole dish. Lay the slices in a casserole dish in one or two layers. Use margarine and butter to create a nice coating of slices of butter.
5. Season the yams. Put lots of white sugar on top. Then sprinkle cinnamon (lots), allspice, and nutmeg, and vanilla. Slice another roll of yams and do the same thing again.
6. Bake the yams for a few minutes

## Notes

- I know this says to not cook the yams all the way, but DO NOT undercook them. Too tender is better than too tough
