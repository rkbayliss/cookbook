## From Rod and Katherine

Mommy makes this pot roast a lot. She made it for Roderick in Cambridge when he broke his arm. This is a modified version of Aunt B's recipe; Mommy adds mushrooms.

> "Can't even tell you the last time Aunt B cooked it." - Mommy

Pictured: preparing the pot roast at Christmas 2022. The roasts were too big for the crock pot, so they were baked instead.

## Ingredients

- White vinegar (to rinse the meat)
- 4 lbs boneless chuck roast (pot roast)
- Worcestershire sauce
- 12 oz white mushrooms
- Lawry's Seasoning Salt (a lot)
- Garlic powder
- Black pepper
- Onion powder
- Italian seasoning
- Flour
- 1 packet onion mushroom soup mix
- 1 can (10.5 oz) cream of mushroom soup
- 1 large yellow onion
- Canola oil

## Preparation

1. Wash the meat, rinsing it with white vinegar.
2. Tenderize the meat with a meat mallet.
3. Coat one side in Worcestershire sauce, then the seasoning salt, garlic powder, onion powder, black pepper, and Italian seasoning.
4. Stab the meat all over with a fork.
5. Flip and repeat steps 3 and 4 on the other side.
6. Coat the roast in flour. You can do this in a plastic grocery bag or a shallow bowl (if using a bowl, put the flour in first).
7. Coat a pan in canola oil and sear the roast on high heat. This is a long sear: 7 to 10 minutes per side.
8. While it sears, slice the onion and mushrooms.
9. After flipping, place the onions over the roast.
10. Move just the roast to the crock pot.
11. Combine the can of mushroom soup with one can of water and the onion mushroom soup mix. This makes a gooooood gravy.
12. Keep cooking the onions in the pan, then add the mushrooms.
13. Pour the onions and mushrooms over the roast. Get all the bits out of the pan! Bits = flavor.
14. Pour the gravy over the roast, onions, and mushrooms.
15. Sprinkle with Italian seasoning.
16. Cook, covered, for at least 4 hours.

## Notes

- The meat will shrink.
- Without a crock pot you could slow cook it in a regular pot for a long time. Mommy doesn't recommend it, but she made do this way in Cambridge when Rod was hurt.
- Consider an Instant Pot to sear and cook everything in one pot.
