Mommy always makes this dish for Christmas. Reese would eat half of the tray.

Mommy's notes:

It's best to buy the block of cheese instead of shredded because shredded cheese has extra anti-clumping stuff to keep it from sticking together

Get the cheese spread evenly to ensure there are crunchy bits.

Sometimes I add a little parmesan which gives it that little something extra

## From Rod and Katherine

## Ingredients

- ½ cup butter
- ½ cup flour
- 60 oz Evaporated milk
- 1 tbsp dried mustard
- ¼ tsp black pepper
- ½ tsp salt
- ¼ tsp nutmeg
- ⅛ tsp smoked paprika
- 12 oz. Regular macaroni noodles (by weight)
- 20-30oz extra sharp cheddar
- 2 handfuls of shredded mozzarella
- ½ lb shredded gruyere
- 1 handful Colby
- 1 handful Monterey Jack
- Additional cheese and smoked paprika for topping

## Preparation

1. Cook macaroni. Salt and boil water, and cook macaroni al dente
2. Make bechamel sauce base. Melt butter over medium heat. Add flour slowly and whisk constantly until combined. Add dried mustard, black pepper, ½ tsp of salt, nutmeg, and paprika. Stir constantly, don't want a lumpy sauce (applicable to any gravy)
3. Preheat oven to 375 ℉
4. Add egg. Scramble one egg, then temper by mixing sauce slowly into the egg while mixing. Then add the egg and sauce mixture into the large sauce pot
5. Add cheese while stirring. Then mix in cooked noodles.
6. Grease bottom of a deep casserole dish and add the macaroni and cheese
7. Top casserole dish with shredded cheddar and smoked paprika.
8. Bake for 25 minutes
