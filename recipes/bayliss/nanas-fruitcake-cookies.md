Mommy remembered Nana having these cookies baked during Christmas, but never saw her bake them.

## Ingredients

- ½ cup butter (room temp)
- 1 cup brown sugar
- 4 small eggs (room temp)
- 3 Tbsp. milk*
- 2 tsp. Baking soda
- 2 ½ cups flour (set aside ¾ cup of this amount)
- 1 tsp. Cloves
- 1 tsp. Nutmeg
- 1 tsp. Cinnamon
- 2 Tbsp. whiskey
- ½ lb cherries (candied)
- 2 ½ cups raisins (golden)
- ½ lb. pineapple (candied) or fruitcake mix
- *this may be condensed milk but we aren't sure

## Preparation

1. Preheat oven to 275 ℉
2. Dredge fruits in ¾ cups flour
3. Cream butter and sugar
4. Add eggs one at a time
5. Add flour (1 ¾ cups) (slowly), soda, and spices
6. Add whiskey and milk
7. Spoon onto sheet with teaspoons
8. Bake for 15 minutes
