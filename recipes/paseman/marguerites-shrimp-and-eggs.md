## From Rod and Katherine

## Ingredients

- shrimp
- salt
- pepper
- eggs
- green onions
- oil (olive oil for stronger taste, canola oil for lighter taste)

## Preparation

1. peel shrimp
2. wash and soak in salt water for 5 min or so
3. drain, add salt, and pepper to taste
4. beat as many eggs as desired in proportion to the shrimp
5. add salt and pepper to egg as desired
6. cut up green onions and mix with eggs
7. medium-high heat, add oil
8. cook shrimp until it turns pink
9. add egg,
10. to make light and thin scramble egg, slide pan to spread egg on pan, use spatula to flip as soon as egg becomes yellow
11. to make thicker more omlette style egg, leave the egg to cook and then flip when cooked halfway
