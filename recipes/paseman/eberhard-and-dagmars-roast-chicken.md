I have always been able to count on Eberhard and Dagmar's hospitality whenever I am in Berlin. They often cook this chicken recipe.

## From Rod and Katherine

## Ingredients

- Fresh Chicken
- Salt
- Red and Black Pepper
- Chili peppers
- Onions
- Tomatoes
- Black Olives
- Olive Oil
- Water

## Preparation

1. Wash and Dry Chicken - inside and out.
2. Salt and pepper it (red and black pepper) inside and out.
3. Put one Chili pepper in cavity
4. Place in Baking Pan
5. Add Onions, Tomatoes, Black Olives, Olive Oil, 1 cup water and remaining peppers to pan
6. Bake at 425 degrees F in a convection oven for 1 hour.

## Notes
