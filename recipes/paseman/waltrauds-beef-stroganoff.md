This was one of my favorite dishes when I was a child and the centerpiece of my 9th or 10th Birthday dinner. My mother asked what I wanted to eat that day, so I chose all of my favorite dishes: Beef Stroganoff, Chocolate Soda, Garlic Bread and Whipped Cream Cake. Mom raised her eyebrows and said nothing, but needless to say, this was WAY too much for a small child's stomach. I only made it 1/2 way through the meal before Mom took mercy and put the rest in the refrigerator.

## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup minced onion
- 1 clove minced garlic
- 1/4 cup butter
- 1 lb ground beef
- 2 tbl flour
- 2 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp pepper
- 1 lb fresh mushrooms or 1 8 oz can sliced mushrooms
- 10 1/2 oz can Campbell's Cream of Chicken soup (undiluted)
- 1 cup sour cream
- 2 tsp minced parsley

## Preparation

1. Saute onion and garlic over medium heat
2. Add meat and brown
3. Add flour, salt, pepper and mushrooms
4. Cook 5 minutes
5. Add soup and simmer uncovered for 10 minutes
6. Stir in sour cream
7. Heat through, sprinkle with parsley, serve with egg noodles.
