This Dish is incredibly simple, and is one of Katherine's favorites. The recipe comes from Grandma Paseman (Waltroud).  The picture is from our 2006 Thanksgiving table.

## From Katherine
I have loved these for as long as I can remember. My favorite way to eat them is as leftovers the day after dinner. You put them on top of rice and eat all the green beans first, then eat the onions and bacon with the rice.

## Ingredients

- 1/2 pot of washed, cleaned (cutting of the stems and tips) Green Beans sliced into 1-1.5" lengths.
- Several Cups Chopped Onion
- Bacon to Taste

## Preparation

1. Cover the Bottom of the pot with bacon.
2. Fry it on medium heat until it is done to your liking (crisp or limp).
3. Pile in the Chopped Onion, then the Green beans on top Reduce heat to low, cover and cook until beans are tender.
4. Mix pot contents and serve.

## Notes

- We used to eat this quite a bit when I was young. Now it is prepared mostly on holidays. A common mistake is to turn the heat too high. This will burn the bacon and bottom layer of beans and onions.
