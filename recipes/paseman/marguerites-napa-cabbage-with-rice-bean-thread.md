Marguerite buys Rice Bean Thread at a Chinese Grocery Store

## From Rod and Katherine

## Ingredients

- one head of napa cabbage
- rice bean thread (soaked in warm water until soft, not cooked through)
- garlic (optional)
- sugar
- salt (optional)
- white pepper (optional)
- chicken broth
- sesame oil (a drop or two, optional)

## Preparation

1. put a little oil in the pan over medium high heat
2. lightly brown a clove of garlic (optional)
3. set to high heat and put in the vegetable and stir fry it for a few minutes
4. set the heat back to medium high
5. put a little sugar and stir fry a couple minutes
6. put a little salt and white pepper (optional)
7. add chicken broth, and cook cabbage until soft
8. add bean thread and cooked until desired softness
9. if you want it soupy, cover
10. if you want it dry, turn up heat to evaporate broth
11. add a drop or two of sesame oil (optional)
