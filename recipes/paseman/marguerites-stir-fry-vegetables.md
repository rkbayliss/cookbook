This is the general mechanism Marguerite uses to create any stir fried vegetable dish. The picture is of Marguerite at a Campsite in Corcovado Park in Costa Rica. She took me there for my 50th birthday.

## From Rod and Katherine

## Ingredients

- green vegetables (bok choy, broccoli, spinach, asparagus, or anything)
- canola oil
- garlic, ginger
- sugar
- salt
- white pepper
- sesame oil (a drop or two)

## Preparation

1. put a little oil in the pan over medium high heat
2. lightly brown a clove of garlic and a small piece of ginger (optional)
3. set to high heat and put in the vegetable and stir fry it for a few minutes
4. set the heat back to medium high
5. put a little sugar and stir fry some
6. then put salt and white pepper to taste
7. if vegetable is not cooked yet and the pan is dry, add a little hot water or chicken broth
8. add a drop or two of sesame oil (optional)
