## Ingredients

- 12 oz Package of Cranberries
- 1 Apple
- 1 Naval Orange without Seeds
- 1 Cup Sugar

## Preparation

1. Peel and dice Fruits
2. Blend and Serve

## Notes

- I have also taken the mixture, added a cup of OJ and some Orange zest, heated it under medium heat on the stove until the cranberries pop (7-10 minutes), then cooled, added 1/2 cup walnuts and served.
