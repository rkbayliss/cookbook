This recipe came off the side of a Ronzinni spaghetti box. When we had kids around, it was probably the second most cooked recipe in our home. I would usually double the amount, cook it on Sunday and it would last most of the week. Katherine and Raymond used to enjoy eating this for breakfast, lunch and dinner.

## Ingredients

- 1 large onion, chopped
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 1/2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1/2 pound ground beef
- 1/2 pound sweet Italian sausage, cut into 1.4" slices
- 1 can (6 oz.) tomato paste
- 1 can (28 oz.) Italian plum tomatoes in juice, crushed
- 1 teaspoon sugar
- 1 teaspoon dried Basil
- 3/4 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 teaspoon thyme
- 1/4 teaspoon pepper
- 1 package (16 oz.) Thin Spaghetti, cooked

## Preparation

1. Saute onion and garlic in hot oil until tender (One variation is to cook the onion until it caramelizes. I also start water boiling for the pasta at this point.)
2. Brown Beef and sausage in same pan.
3. Stir in tomato paste, tomatoes, sugar and seasonings.
4. Bring to a boil. Reduce heat, simmer uncovered 20 minutes.
5. Serve sauce over hot pasta.

## Notes

- It is fine to use Italian style (Basil added in the can) crushed plum tomatoes as well.
