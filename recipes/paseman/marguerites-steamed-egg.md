## Ingredients

- as many eggs as desired
- same amount of chicken broth

## Preparation

1. beat the eggs
2. put in pyrex dish
3. add as much volume of chicken broth to plate
4. mix well
5. put in steamer when water is boiling
6. steam for about 10 minutes
7. egg is done when it solidifies

## Notes

- Marguerite's Sweet version of this: 1 Egg 2/3 Cup Milk 2 tbl Sugar little vanilla
