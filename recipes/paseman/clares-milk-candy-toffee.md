Mrs. Hsu would make this for Christmas. I could always count on a jar as a present. She would also send a jar every year to my father, who had a big sweet tooth. In fact, one story my father would often recite was when he was a little boy visiting his father's father. He brought a bag of jelly beans for the summer, which his grandmother promptly confiscated. Thereafter throughout the whole summer, the woman would give my father one jellybean a day. The thing that really got him upset was that they were HIS jellybeans. Although he did not eat much in general, he always had a jar of jellybeans sitting around the house as I grew up, and so especially liked this yearly gift from Clare.

## From Rod and Katherine

## Ingredients

- 12 oz Sugar
- 4 oz Water
- 1 stick butter, cut into 8 pieces
- 1 can condensed milk
- Added flavorings such as Skor bars or almond extract (optional)

## Preparation

1. Dissolve the sugar in the water
2. Add 1/8th stick of butter, watch till it completely dissolves 2a. (Optional) if you want to add coffee, golden syrup, cocoa, malt or other flavor, add now
3. Add 1 can condensed milk.
4. Stir for 45 minutes (no stopping!) until it "leaves the bowl" using medium or medium/low heat. As the candy thickens and start to leave the pot, add vanilla or almond extract (1tsp)
5. Pour the goo into the buttered container; Let sit for 5 minutes
6. Score the candy with a butter knife (cut into 4 pieces); flip over to cool on both sides Note: Candy is most delicious in this semi-molten semi-cooled state.
7. After about 10 minutes, completely cut the candies and store in a jar
