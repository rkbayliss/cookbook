This is not as good as Marguerite's Steamed Fish, but it is pretty good and a -lot- faster and easier to fix. Fresh fish is important, even if you have to fight to keep it in the pan.

## Ingredients

**Sauce**

- 1 tbl ginger,
- 1 tbl garlic,
- 2 green onions,
- 1/4 cup rice wine,
- 1/4 cup soy sauce,
- 1/4 cup water,
- 1 tbl rice vinegar and
- 1 tbl sugar.

**Fish**

- Catfish (or other fish) Fillets

## Preparation

1. Mix and bring the sauce to a boil in a large pan on med-high
2. Poach the fish fillets for 5 minutes.

## Notes

- You can substitute similar fresh fish like bass or trout for catfish. Serve over Rice.
