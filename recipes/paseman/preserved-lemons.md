These lemons are used in making Tangine Chicken.

## Ingredients

- 3-4 firm lemons
- 4 tablespoons salt
- Brine soulution made from dissolving 1 rounded tablespoon salt in 500ml (2 cups) hot water.

## Preparation

1. Cut the lemons into quarters and rub a level teaspoon of salt all over each piece.
2. Pack these pieces very tightly in a clean jam jar.
3. Make the brine solution and cool it. Pour over the lemons making sure they are fully submerged.
4. A piece of crumpled backing or greaseproof paper can be pushed down on top of them. Screw on a lid and store in the refrigerator.
5. Use after two weeks.

## Notes

- This should not be eaten alone! It is generally used in other recipes.
