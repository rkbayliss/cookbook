My Mother used to make this for me on Weekends. It is simple to make and very delicious. The picture below was taken of her and me around 1957 or 1958.

## Ingredients

- 1 Egg
- 1 Tablespoon Sugar
- 1 Tablespoon Whole Milk
- 1 Pat Butter
- (Optional) 1 Teaspoon Jam

## Preparation

1. Beat Egg, Sugar and Milk together in a cup.
2. Melt butter on medium heat in 6" skillet (preferably Cast Iron)
3. Pour egg mixture in skillet and Flip when edges crust and center is solid.
4. After pancake is cooked, place on a plate, spread a line of jam in the middle and roll into a tube.
5. Best served hot.

## Notes

- Key to the recipe is the butter.
