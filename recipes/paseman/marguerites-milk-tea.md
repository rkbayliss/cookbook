Marguerite likes this a lot. She used to drink it in Hong Kong.

## From Rod and Katherine

## Ingredients

- 1 Teabag
- 500 ml (Beer Mug full) Boiling Water
- 1 Tbl Condensed Milk (or "to taste")

## Preparation

1. Put Teabag in Beer Mug
2. Pour in Boiling water
3. Let steep 2-3 minutes
4. Remove Teabag
5. Stir in Condensed Milk

## Notes

- These Beverage recipes are trivial. However I always wondered what my Mom drank. Now you know what your Mom did.
